For the work only your people can do.

Knowing your customers. Caring about the details. Making the next thing better. That’s where your people belong. We build and run the automation that gives them more room for it.

Small team. Close to the work.

Quavok is an early-stage company with two founders. We think good people spend too much of their day moving information around. We want to help them get back to the work they care about.

So we take that job. We design custom AI agents, workflows and integrations around how a business already operates, and then we run them. Our customers explain the work and approve what it does; they never manage the technology.

We're working toward our first client deployments. We won't pretend otherwise: there are no logos or case studies on this site yet, because we don't publish claims we can't back up. What you get today is direct access to the people who will build and run your automation.

How we operate

A few things we hold ourselves to.

Honest about fit

If a process isn't a good candidate for automation, we'll tell you, even if that means there's no project.

Custom, not catalogue

We build around how your business already operates. We don't sell prebuilt packs you have to adapt to.

People stay in charge

You approve what an automation is allowed to do. Exceptions and judgment calls go to people.

We own the running

Building is half the job. Monitoring, fixing and improving what we built is the other half, and it's ours.
