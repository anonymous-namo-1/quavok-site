# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and teams considering help with repetitive operations. They explain their process and decide whether to discuss a custom automation with Quavok.

## Product Purpose

Quavok designs, deploys and runs custom AI agents, workflows and integrations. Automation takes repetitive busywork off the team's plate so people can focus on customers, craft, growth and what actually matters.

## Positioning

Fully managed service. Customers never build, configure, deploy or maintain agents. Quavok scopes and builds the system; customers approve the work and important decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rocket is the conversational layer over automations Quavok built. New-automation requests go to the Quavok team. The website's Rocket is a scripted conversation: no AI backend, no connected customer automations and no live support. The paid proactive add-on and desktop/mobile apps are in development.

The contact form only prepares a mailto draft to hello@quavok.com. Keep its TODO hook and never claim a message was sent. Existing published contact information may remain. Unknown facts retain visible PLACEHOLDER markers.

Existing Next.js static export remains the delivery mechanism. The owner has removed the requirement that the experience stay lightweight/static; richer rendering may be adopted if it materially improves the design. No deployment changes, pushes, merges or PRs are authorised in this task. Local commit is authorised.

## Brand Commitments

Quavok and existing brand assets remain. Voice: human, confident, people-first, short sentences. Avoid employee-replacement framing, filler and invented evidence. Dark by default, warm Charcoal, Off-white Beige and Electric Purple. Sora display and self-hosted fonts stay.

## Evidence on Hand

research/plan.md, research/grok-x.md, research/sol-web.md and research/inspiration.md. Real brand marks and published contact details. Illustrative workflows and scripted conversations are labelled as such. No testimonials, customer logos, metrics, prices, certifications, founder biographies or history may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- The customer requests and approves; Quavok handles the technology.
- Demonstrations show a mechanism, not fictional business results.
- Rocket's companion and unreleased capabilities are labelled beside the interaction.
- The main action is Discuss Your Workflow or Book a Discovery Call.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard controls, visible focus, compact touch interactions, reduced-motion finished states and usable 390px/1440px layouts are explicit requirements.
