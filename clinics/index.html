Safety first

Built with clear limits.

A clinic's front desk carries more responsibility than a shop's, so these rules are part of every clinic setup.

No medical advice

It never diagnoses, suggests medicines or comments on reports. Symptoms and treatment questions go straight to your team, and urgent-sounding messages tell the patient to seek emergency care.

Only your approved answers

Fees, timings and policies come from a list you approve. If something isn't on it, the assistant says your team will reply instead of guessing.

Honest and private

It says it's an AI assistant when asked. It doesn't ask for reports or health records in chat, and you can ask us to export or delete your data at any time.

FAQ

Questions clinics ask us.

Will it ever give medical advice?

No. It's set up for front-desk tasks only. Any question about symptoms, medicines, reports or treatment is passed to your team, and the patient is told a person will reply.

We already have a receptionist. Why add this?

It doesn't replace your receptionist. It covers the hours and moments they can't: after closing, Sundays, and when they're busy with patients at the counter. Your receptionist can take over any chat at any time.

Will patients know it's an AI?

Yes, if they ask. It never pretends to be a person or a doctor.

Does it work with our clinic software?

Often, yes, either through a calendar or Google Sheet, or on Managed Ops through browser automation. We check your software on the call before we promise anything.

Do we need a new WhatsApp number?

Usually not. Automated replies run on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and we help you move your existing clinic number onto it where possible.

What about patient data?

Each clinic gets its own separate assistant and data. It collects only what's needed to book (name, phone, preferred time) and doesn't ask for health records in chat. See our Privacy Policy.
