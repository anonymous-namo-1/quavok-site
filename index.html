The problem

Repeat WhatsApp work waits while you are with a client.

Enquiries, intake, status checks and follow-ups sit in the same chat. By the time someone replies, the person has often moved on.

Slow replies lose the next step

People message more than one business and continue with whoever answers. A campaign enquiry, a site brief or a booking request can all be in that queue.

Handoffs and follow-ups slip

Approvals, revision updates, reminders and rebooking nudges depend on someone remembering to send them.

The same questions, after hours

Scope, fees, status, timings, "kal slot hai?". The messages are different. The pile-up at the end of the day is the same.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes, if they ask. The assistant never pretends to be a person. It says it's your AI assistant and offers to connect them to your team.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

Usually not. Automated replies run on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and we help you set up your existing business number on it. If that isn't possible, we'll talk you through the options on the call.

What if the bot doesn't know an answer?

It tells the customer someone will get back to them and alerts you straight away. It won't make up prices or promises.

How long does setup take?

We start within 2 working days of payment, and a typical setup is live in 7 to 14 working days, depending on how quickly we get your details and approvals. Part of that is Meta's business verification, which we guide you through.

Which businesses is this for?

Businesses with repeat work on WhatsApp. Marketing agencies can use it for campaign enquiries, data intake, onboarding, approvals and reporting. Website agencies can use it for prospect qualification, scope information, onboarding, revisions, status and routine support. Salons, coaching, repair shops and clinics remain examples, including bookings where that is the job. Each assistant runs only the tasks you approve.

What does Managed Ops include?

Managed Ops is ₹19,999 a month plus a ₹24,999 one-time setup. Yearly is ₹1,99,990, and the setup fee is waived. It runs up to 6 workflows you agree in advance, and up to 4,000 AI conversations a month. Examples include client intake, onboarding, approvals, status updates, reporting and handoff. Apps with no API can be included only after we validate them. You approve the operating scope, and a person reviews anything outside it. It is not a promise to automate every task.

What will the assistant not do?

It will not make medical, legal, financial or HR decisions, and it will not complete consequential transactions, such as taking a payment, issuing a refund or agreeing a contract, on its own. It answers and acts only from the operating scope you approve. We do not guarantee leads, revenue, bookings or hours saved, and we do not promise an integration we have not checked.

What about my customers' data?

Each client gets their own separate assistant and data. We never ask for your passwords in chat, and you can ask us to export or delete your data at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel with 30 days' notice, and pilot plans can be cancelled anytime.
