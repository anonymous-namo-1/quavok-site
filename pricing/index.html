What the plans cover

Audit (₹2,999 one-time): a call and a short report on which tasks are worth automating. Any hours figure in that report is an estimate of time those tasks take today, not a promise of savings.

Starter (₹4,999/month, ₹9,999 setup, or ₹49,990/year with setup waived): one workflow you agree, such as an enquiry assistant or bookings with reminders. 500 AI conversations a month.

Growth (₹9,999/month, ₹14,999 setup, or ₹99,990/year with setup waived): up to 3 agreed workflows, such as answers, intake or bookings, and follow-ups. 1,500 AI conversations a month.

Managed Ops (₹19,999/month + ₹24,999 setup, or ₹1,99,990/year with setup waived): up to 6 workflows you agree, and 4,000 AI conversations a month. The work can include client intake, onboarding, approvals, status updates, reporting and handoff. Browser automation of one or two apps that have no API is included only after we validate each app. You approve the operating scope before go-live, and a person reviews anything outside it. This is a tailored setup of agreed tasks, not a promise to automate every task.

Marketing agencies can use these workflows for campaign enquiries, data intake, onboarding, approvals and reporting. Website agencies can use them for prospect qualification, scope information, onboarding, revisions, status and routine support. Salons, coaching, repair shops and clinics remain examples, including appointment workflows. The clinic page is a front-desk example that does not give medical advice. Appointment work is not limited to clinics.

None of the plans make medical, legal, financial or HR decisions, or complete consequential transactions, on their own. We do not guarantee leads, revenue, bookings or other business results, and we do not promise an integration we have not checked.

WhatsApp message fees charged by Meta are passed through at cost and shown separately. AI conversations above your plan's monthly limit are billed at ₹2 each, or you can move to a bigger plan. See the home page for the same plan limits.

How to pay: after we agree your plan, pay on our payment page. You get an invoice for every payment.
